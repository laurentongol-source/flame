Federations of Labourers, Artisans, Mages, and Engineers

"Keep the F.L.A.M.E of fairness burning!"

Campaign: The Right to Rest (and Respawn)

We are tired of being mages, smiths, engineers, and potion brewers are working long hours without proper breaks. We're not asking for magic - just a moment to eat our stew in peace.

Our 3 Main rules:

  1. The 4-Hour Bed Rule Every worker gets a break every 4 hours. Even during dragon seiges
  2. Universal Bedroll access Free, flameproof bedrolls for all guild-certified workers.
  3. Respawn Leave If you're ressurected due to unsafe working conditions, you get the day off. No arguments.

Slogans you'll see around

  • "Breaks before Blades!"
  • "No Stew, No Spells!"
  • "Stop the Grind - Nap Time now!"
