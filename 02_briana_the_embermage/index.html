Case Study: Brina the Apprentice

Brina's Story and thoughts

Brina is a 14-year-old apprentice mage from Emberhill. She's part of the Arcane Engineers subgroup of F.L.A.M.E.

Brina's Main Issues

  • No sleep schedule
  • No lunch breaks
  • No protection for magical burnout

Brina's Statement

"I was expected to stay up three nights in a row enchanting scrolls for a royal gala. No one asked if I needed sleep, food, or time to recharge."
